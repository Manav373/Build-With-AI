# 🌾 KrishiAI — Farmer Architecture & Capabilities

## 1. Overview
The **Farmer Module** in KrishiAI provides AI-driven agronomic intelligence, satellite monitoring, crop price tracking, and direct marketplace access.

## 2. Key Features
- **AI Agronomist Chat & Vision Diagnosis**: Multimodal crop disease detection via Gemini Vision and Groq.
- **Satellite Health Telemetry**: Google Earth Engine NDVI vegetation index and soil moisture analytics.
- **ML Crop Yield Prediction & Recommendations**: Random Forest models predicting yield per acre and best crop recommendations.
- **Live Mandi Rates & Proximity Navigation**: Interactive APMC price tracking and geo-located mandi mapping.
- **Sell Produce to Verified Buyers**: Direct crop selling to authenticated institutional buyers and vendors without middleman exploitation.

## 3. Directory Layout
```
farmer/
├── profile/        # Farmer domain profile schemas and sample datasets
├── files/          # Land records, certificates, and crop image uploads
```
