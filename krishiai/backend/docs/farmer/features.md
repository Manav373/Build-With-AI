# Farmer Module Features & Capabilities

The Farmer module of KrishiAI delivers an end-to-end intelligent agronomist assistant:

1. **Multimodal Crop Doctor**:
   - Leaf image disease diagnosis using computer vision with treatment recommendations and biological alternatives.
   - Multilingual voice queries in Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and English.

2. **Real-time Mandi Intelligence**:
   - Integration with Data.gov.in AGMARKNET price feeds for 50+ major crops.
   - Proximity routing to nearest APMC yards with road distance and travel time.

3. **Hyper-Local Weather & GEE Satellite Analytics**:
   - Live Open-Meteo & OpenWeatherMap alerts with agricultural advisories.
   - Google Earth Engine NDVI, NDRE, moisture, and temperature analysis.

4. **Kisan Call Center AI**:
   - Inbound & outbound conversational voice agent powered by Vapi AI.

5. **Marketplace Direct Selling**:
   - Browse buying requirements posted by verified vendors.
   - Submit crop bids, participate in transparent price negotiations, and track pickup orders.
